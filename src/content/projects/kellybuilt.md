My web-development brand site — designed and built end to end to prove the point by being fast, accessible, and polished. Live.

## The goal
A marketing site for my development work where the site itself is the portfolio piece: cinematic but fast, fully accessible, and conversion-focused.

## What I built
A Next.js 15 / React 19 site with an animated hero, a synchronized before/after slideshow, an SVG line-drawing process timeline (horizontal on desktop, vertical on mobile), a categorized 20-question FAQ, transparent productized pricing, integrated Cal.com booking, and a service-area map. It's privacy-conscious by design — no tracking cookies and business-only contact details.

## How it works
Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind v4, and Framer Motion, deployed on Vercel. Images use Next/Image with automatic AVIF/WebP conversion and responsive sizing; fonts are subset with display swap. Full Open Graph and Twitter metadata, an auto-generated sitemap and robots file, and Search Console verification round out the SEO.

## Impact
Live and mobile-first indexed, with strong Lighthouse scores on mobile: 100 SEO, 100 Best Practices, 93 Performance, and 91 Accessibility. End-to-end proof of design, performance, and accessibility.
